Livery Studio

A livery editor for Assetto Corsa Competizione. You paint straight onto the 3D car model — no UV map to look at — and you get back an archive in the shape the game expects.

Support

A question, a bug or a wish — write to nasriddinov.islom.19@gmail.com. I answer in English and in Russian.

To sort it out faster, please include:

  • which device and which system version
  • what you did, and what happened instead
  • if it is about a car model — what format it is in and where it came from

Where to get a car model

The game keeps its cars in the closed .kn5 format, so the app cannot read them. You need a model in .obj or .glb. The app has a screen that explains where to look.

Privacy

The app collects no data and sends nothing anywhere. The details are in the privacy policy.
