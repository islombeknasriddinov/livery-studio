Privacy Policy — Livery Studio

Effective date: 5 September 2026.

One policy for both versions of the app, iOS and Android. Where they behave differently, it is said so.

In short

Livery Studio collects no data about you: no analytics, no advertising, no device identifiers. The app sends nothing anywhere on its own.

What is stored, and where

Everything you do in the app stays on your device:

  • the car models you added
  • liveries: layers, covers, team and driver names, race number
  • the images you placed as decals
  • the archives built for the game

The developer has no access to these files — the app has no server.

On iOS the files live in the app's documents folder: you can see them in the Files app, and they go into your device backup if you have one enabled.

On Android the files live in the app's internal storage. Other apps cannot see them, and they do not go into a backup: app data backup is turned off (allowBackup="false").

Deleting the app deletes all of these files with it.

Network access

The app makes no network requests — not when parsing a model, not while painting, not when building the archive.

On Android the app requests no permissions at all: no internet access, no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no photo library. You pick the model and the images through the system file picker, which needs none. The permission list on the app's Google Play page is empty — you can check.

Sharing

When you build an archive and choose where to send it, the file is handed over by the system in the way you picked: AirDrop, Files, email, a messenger. That is your action and your choice of recipient — the app only hands the file to the system.

Purchases

Neither version has any purchases: everything is free and unlimited. The app talks to neither the App Store nor Google Play about purchases, and it never sees or stores payment details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, a new effective date will appear here.
